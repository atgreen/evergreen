# Code of Conduct

Evergreen Common Lisp welcomes people who want to improve the implementation,
its documentation, and the surrounding Common Lisp ecosystem. This code of
conduct applies in the repository, issue tracker, review discussions, project
chat, and other spaces managed by the project.

## Expected conduct

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code with patience, honesty, and technical care;
- criticize behavior, evidence, and designs without attacking people;
- give others room to ask basic questions and revise mistaken conclusions;
- respect stated names, pronouns, boundaries, and accessibility needs;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest and the provenance of contributed
  work; and
- protect private information and report security-sensitive findings through
  the private process in `SECURITY.md`.

Disagreement is normal. Strong technical criticism is welcome when it is
specific, supported by evidence, and directed at the work. Maintainers may ask
participants to narrow a discussion, provide a reproducer, or move unrelated
work to another issue.

## Unacceptable conduct

The following behavior is not accepted:

- harassment, threats, stalking, or intimidation;
- discriminatory or demeaning comments;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- sustained disruption, trolling, or knowingly false claims about another
  participant;
- publishing private information without permission;
- evading moderation or encouraging someone else to violate this policy; or
- retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to Anthony Green at
`green@moxielogic.com`. Include links or copies of relevant material and any
immediate safety concern. Do not use a public issue when doing so would expose
the reporter or another participant.

The maintainer will review reports as privately and promptly as practical,
consider the context and pattern of behavior, and avoid unnecessary disclosure
of the reporter's identity. Possible responses include a private correction,
an explicit warning, removal of content, temporary limits on participation, or
a permanent ban from project spaces. A maintainer who is directly involved in
a report should seek a neutral third party to review it when one is available.

Good-faith reports will not be penalized. Deliberately false or retaliatory
reports may themselves violate this policy.

## Scope and changes

This policy governs participation in project-managed spaces. It may also apply
to public conduct elsewhere when that conduct creates a credible safety risk or
materially undermines a person's ability to participate in the project.

The maintainer may update this policy as the community grows. Material changes
will be recorded in the repository history.
